Privacy Policy

Noku collects no data. Your progress stays on your device, and nothing leaves it.

What Noku stores

To let you pick up where you left off, Noku saves a few things on your iPhone:

  • The puzzle you are playing, including your entries and notes
  • The difficulty level you chose
  • How many puzzles you have completed at each level

This stays on your device. Deleting Noku deletes it. Like other app data, it may be included in your iPhone or iCloud backups, which Apple handles under your own settings.

What Noku does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet.
  • It has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
  • It contains no third-party code that could collect data.
  • It does not share or sell anything, because it has nothing to share.

Children

Noku is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
