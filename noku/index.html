Questions

How do I change the difficulty?

Tap the level name at the top of the screen. You can pick Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert there, and see how many puzzles you have completed at each level.

How do I start a new puzzle?

Tap New Game at the top right.

How do notes work?

Turn on Notes, then tap digits to pencil them into a cell. When you place a digit, the matching notes in its row, column and box clear themselves.

Why won’t a digit go in?

Noku turns away any digit that already appears in the same row, column or box, and briefly shows the cells it clashes with.

How do I start the same puzzle over?

Press and hold Undo.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Noku keeps your progress only on your device. It comes along when you restore your iPhone from a backup, but it does not sync between devices.
